pastport

Keep every ticket.

pastport finds the ticket emails in your Gmail and turns every concert, game, festival and fight night you've been to into a collectible card. Add a photo or clip from the night, seal it, and share it with friends.

  • Find your nights. Read-only Gmail access, and only emails that look like tickets.
  • Seal your cards. Each night becomes a graded slab, from Common to Legendary.
  • Share what you choose. Friends only see cards you've opened and posted.

pastport is in private beta on iPhone through TestFlight.
